## Installation

```bash
bunx dreamy add field
```

## Usage

Field connects a form control to its label, hint description, and error messages, powered by [UI SDK Field](https://ui-sdk.dev/docs/components/field).

<Wrapper>
	<Field.Root>
		<Field.Label>Username</Field.Label>
		<Input placeholder="Enter your username" />
		<Field.Hint>This is the username you will use to login</Field.Hint>
	</Field.Root>
</Wrapper>

```tsx
<Field.Root>
	<Field.Label>Username</Field.Label>
	<Input placeholder="Enter your username" />
	<Field.Hint>This is the username you will use to login</Field.Hint>
</Field.Root>
```

### Required

A required indicator is automatically added to the label when `required` is set on `Field.Root` or on the control.

<Wrapper>
	<Field.Root required>
		<Field.Label>Username</Field.Label>
		<Input placeholder="Enter your username" />
	</Field.Root>
</Wrapper>

```tsx
<Field.Root required>
	<Field.Label>Username</Field.Label>
	<Input placeholder="Enter your username" />
</Field.Root>
```

### Invalid

Controls are marked invalid when `invalid` is passed, and `Field.Error` becomes visible.

<Wrapper>
	<Field.Root invalid>
		<Field.Label>Username</Field.Label>
		<Input placeholder="Enter your username" defaultValue="x" />
		<Field.Error>This username is too short!</Field.Error>
	</Field.Root>
</Wrapper>

```tsx
<Field.Root invalid>
	<Field.Label>Username</Field.Label>
	<Input placeholder="Enter your username" defaultValue="x" />
	<Field.Error>This username is too short!</Field.Error>
</Field.Root>
```

### Disabled

The entire field and its control are disabled when `disabled` is passed.

<Wrapper>
	<Field.Root disabled>
		<Field.Label>Username</Field.Label>
		<Input placeholder="Enter your username" />
	</Field.Root>
</Wrapper>

```tsx
<Field.Root disabled>
	<Field.Label>Username</Field.Label>
	<Input placeholder="Enter your username" />
</Field.Root>
```

### Using Field components as props

You can pass `label`, `hint`, and `error` directly to `Field.Root` as shorthand props. `error` is shown when `invalid` is `true` or native validation fails, replacing `hint` while invalid.

<Wrapper>
	<Field.Root
		label="Username"
		hint="This is the username you will use to login"
		error="This username is too short!"
	>
		<Input placeholder="Enter your username" />
	</Field.Root>
</Wrapper>

```tsx
<Field.Root
	label="Username"
	hint="This is the username you will use to login"
	error="This username is too short!"
>
	<Input placeholder="Enter your username" />
</Field.Root>
```

### Horizontal

Use `orientation="horizontal"` to place the label beside the control.

<Wrapper>
	<Field.Root orientation="horizontal" maxW="sm">
		<Field.Label>Username</Field.Label>
		<Input placeholder="Enter your username" />
	</Field.Root>
</Wrapper>

```tsx
<Field.Root orientation="horizontal" maxW="sm">
	<Field.Label>Username</Field.Label>
	<Input placeholder="Enter your username" />
</Field.Root>
```
