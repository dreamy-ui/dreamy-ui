## Installation

```bash
bunx dreamy add tooltip
```

## Usage

Tooltip behavior is powered by [UI SDK Tooltip](https://ui-sdk.dev/docs/components/tooltip) with Dreamy styling and top-layer positioning. Use `open`, `defaultOpen`, and `onOpenChange` for controlled state. `hasArrow` defaults to `true`.

Base usage of a Tooltip.

<Wrapper>
	<Tooltip content="I am visible on hover">
		<Text w="fit-content">Hover me</Text>
	</Tooltip>
</Wrapper>

```tsx
<Tooltip content="I am visible on hover">
	<Text>Hover me</Text>
</Tooltip>
```

### Open and close delay

You can control the open and close delay of the tooltip with `delay` and `closeDelay`.

<Wrapper>
	<Tooltip content="I am visible on hover after 1000 milliseconds" delay={1000}>
		<Text w="fit-content">Hover me (1s open)</Text>
	</Tooltip>
	<Tooltip content="I am closed after 1000 milliseconds" closeDelay={1000}>
		<Text w="fit-content">Hover me (1s close)</Text>
	</Tooltip>
</Wrapper>

```tsx
<Tooltip content="I am visible on hover after 1000 milliseconds" delay={1000}>
    <Text>Hover me (1s open)</Text>
</Tooltip>
<Tooltip content="I am closed after 1000 milliseconds" closeDelay={1000}>
    <Text>Hover me (1s close)</Text>
</Tooltip>
```

### Press and dismissal behavior

Use `closeOnPress={false}` to keep the tooltip open when the trigger is pressed (for example, a copy button), `toggleable` to allow press-to-toggle on touch devices, or `onOpenChange` with `details.cancel()` to control dismissal reasons such as `"escape-key"`.

<Wrapper>
	<Tooltip content="I stay open on press" closeOnPress={false}>
		<Text w="fit-content">Keep open on press</Text>
	</Tooltip>
	<Tooltip content="Tap or press to toggle" toggleable>
		<Text w="fit-content">Toggleable</Text>
	</Tooltip>
</Wrapper>

```tsx
<Tooltip content="I stay open on press" closeOnPress={false}>
    <Text>Keep open on press</Text>
</Tooltip>
<Tooltip content="Tap or press to toggle" toggleable>
    <Text>Toggleable</Text>
</Tooltip>
```

### Arrow

You can control the arrow of the tooltip by using `hasArrow` and `arrowSize`.

<Wrapper>
	<Tooltip content="I have no arrow" hasArrow={false}>
		<Text w="fit-content">No arrow</Text>
	</Tooltip>
	<Tooltip content="I have an arrow" arrowSize={15}>
		<Text w="fit-content">Arrow size 15</Text>
	</Tooltip>
</Wrapper>

```tsx
<Tooltip content="I have no arrow" hasArrow={false}>
    <Text>No arrow</Text>
</Tooltip>
<Tooltip content="I have an arrow" arrowSize={15}>
    <Text>Arrow size 15</Text>
</Tooltip>
```

### Placement and top-layer stacking

Tooltip uses UI SDK's top-layer positioning and renders in place without requiring a Portal wrapper. Configure placement with `side`, `align`, `sideOffset`, or the `positioning` prop. Use `container` only when targeting a custom container such as a shadow root or iframe.

<Wrapper>
	<Tooltip content="Top-layer tooltip" side="bottom">
		<Text w="fit-content">Hover me</Text>
	</Tooltip>
</Wrapper>

```tsx
<Tooltip content="Top-layer tooltip" side="bottom">
	<Text>Hover me</Text>
</Tooltip>
```
